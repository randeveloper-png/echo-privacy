The Silent Journey · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

ECHO: The Silent Journey ("the game", "the app") is published by Randev Studio ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the app uses and why. If you have any question, write to us at randevloper@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (levels reached, settings such as sound) on your device only. We do not run any server for the game, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not receive your progress or your name, email address, phone number or location from the game itself.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. Two kinds of ads are used: a full-screen ad that may appear between attempts, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to continue a level.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, for example your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, app and ad interaction data, and approximate location derived from the IP address. We do not receive this data and we do not sell it. How Google handles it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Your choices (consent)

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform when you start it. Ads are only requested after the rules for your region allow it. When a privacy choices option is required for your region, you can change your answer at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app.

On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Internet permission

The app uses an internet connection only to load ads and to show the consent message. The game itself can be played offline.

5. Children

ECHO is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

6. Security and retention

Because we do not collect your personal data ourselves, we do not store it. Data handled by Google is kept according to Google's own policies. Uninstalling the app removes the saved progress from your device.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as access, deletion or objection. Since we hold no personal data about you, the relevant requests are generally for Google, through your Google Account and the controls above. You can still contact us at the address below for anything related to the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above.
